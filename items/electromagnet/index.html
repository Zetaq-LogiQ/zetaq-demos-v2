Electromagnet Bench

Can we use electric current to make a magnet? Close the circuit, choose the turns, the cells and the core, and watch the compass and the iron paper clips.

  • Wire + compass: a wire stretched over a magnetic compass, a cell holder and a switch.
  • Electromagnet: a paper-tube coil (25 to 100 turns), an iron nail or other core, 1 to 4 cells, two compasses at ends A and B, and a dish of iron paper clips.
  • 6 tasks, in any order. Press Record to note what you see.
In a real lab, do not keep the wires connected to the cell for more than a few seconds; otherwise, the cell may weaken quickly.
